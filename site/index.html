How it works

One drop in. Clips out.

The whole run happens inside one engine on your computer, and the app shows every step live.

  2. Video or link

    Drop a file, paste a link from YouTube, TikTok, X or Vimeo, or let a watch folder feed it.

  3. Extract audio

    ffmpeg pulls the sound off the file. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  4. Transcribe

    whisper.cpp is compiled into the engine and times every single word, offline.

  5. Pick + judge

    Your LLM or the offline scorer ranks every stretch, then a judge re-scores the candidates.

  6. Track the speaker

    Face tracking plans the camera: hold, follow, and cut where the source cuts.

  7. Render

    Clips render side by side on the GPU, captions burned in, audio at −14 LUFS.

  8. Clips

    9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9, each with .srt captions and an upload kit.
